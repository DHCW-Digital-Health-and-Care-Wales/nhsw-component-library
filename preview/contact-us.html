Contact us

If you have a question, feedback or idea for improvement, email design.system@wales.nhs.uk.

We improve the design system through evidence, feedback and contributions from teams across Digital Health and Care Wales.

Contact us to:

  • suggest changes or request new components and patterns based on user need
  • share user research, accessibility findings or lessons learned from your service
  • ask for help using the design system
