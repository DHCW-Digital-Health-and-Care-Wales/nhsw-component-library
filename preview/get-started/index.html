Get started

The Digital Health and Care Wales Design System helps teams design and build consistent, accessible digital services.

Use the design system when creating a new service, improving an existing service, or exploring ideas through prototyping and user research.

Choose how you want to prototype

Prototyping helps you explore ideas, test assumptions and learn from users before investing time in development.

Prototype in Figma

Figma is useful for exploring ideas, creating design concepts and sharing work with stakeholders. It allows teams to create and test designs quickly before moving into development.

Digital Health and Care Wales provides staff access to Figma depending on your needs. Email dhcw.ucd@wales.nhs.uk for more information.

Read more about setting up Figma prototypes

Prototype in code

Coded prototypes use the same components that teams use in real services. They provide a realistic experience for users and can help you identify usability issues earlier.

Coded prototypes are particularly useful when testing complex interactions, journeys and form-based services.

Read more about setting up code prototypes

What you'll find in the design system

Styles

Styles define the visual foundations of Digital Health and Care Wales services, including colours and typography. Use styles to create interfaces that feel familiar, accessible and consistent.

See all styles

Components

Components are reusable interface elements such as buttons, form inputs, tables and cards. Each component includes guidance, accessibility considerations and examples of when and how to use it.

See all components

Accessibility and user research

The design system is built to support the design of accessible services. Components and patterns are reviewed against Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA and refined through feedback.

Teams should still test their own services with users and make sure designs meet the needs of the people who will use them.

Contribute and get support

The design system is shaped by the feedback of teams across Digital Health and Care Wales and NHS wales. Help us improve the design system by suggesting changes and contributing patterns, components and guidance.

Email design.system@wales.nhs.uk to share your feedback.
