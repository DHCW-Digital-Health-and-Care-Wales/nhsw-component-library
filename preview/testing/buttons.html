Manual test scenarios
Given a primary, secondary, exceptional or warning button. Then its clickable area is comfortably large, matching its visible size.
Given the warning (red) and exceptional (amber) buttons sitting near a primary button. Then each has a clear text label so its purpose doesn't depend on the colour.
Given any button variant. Then it's clearly visible against a white page background.
Given a primary button that submits a form. When a user double-clicks it, or the network is slow. Then the information is not sent twice.
Given the smaller button variant. Then the text stays legible and it's still comfortable to tap on mobile.
Given a group of buttons. When viewed on a mobile-width screen. Then they stack or wrap without overlapping or getting cut off.
Given a group of buttons. When a user tabs through them. Then each one shows a clear yellow focus outline as it's reached.
