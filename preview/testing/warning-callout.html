Manual test scenarios
Given a warning callout. Then the heading and body text are easy to read against their background.
Given a warning callout. Then it combines an icon and text with the yellow colouring, not colour by itself. And hidden "Warning" text is present for screen reader users.
Given the warning icon. Then it has clear contrast against its background.
Given a page with one or more warning callouts. Then they're used at the frequency described in the doc page's guidance, not overused.
