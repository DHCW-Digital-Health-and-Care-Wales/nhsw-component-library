Manual test scenarios
Given a panel with typical content. When the page is zoomed to 400%. Then the text doesn't clip, overlap, or get cut off.
Given the green (default) and navy panel variants. Then each has a clear heading so its purpose doesn't depend on the colour.
Given the no-icon panel variant, large and small. Then the heading and body text alone are enough to understand the message.
Given the smaller panel variant. Then the icon and text stay proportionate and easy to read.
