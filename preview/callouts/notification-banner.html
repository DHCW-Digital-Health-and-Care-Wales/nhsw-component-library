Notification banner

Use a notification banner to tell the user about something they need to know about, but that is not directly related to the page content.

Default notification banner

Use the default notification banner to tell the user about important information or a pending action.

Important

You have 7 days left to send your application. View application.

Default banner with more content

Important

Your GP surgery has moved

Riverside Medical Practice has moved to a new location. The new address is 123 High Street, Cardiff, CF10 1AA.

Contact the surgery on 029 2000 0000 if you have any questions.

Success notification banner

Use the success variant to confirm that something the user did was successful. The success banner uses role="alert" so it is immediately announced by screen readers.

Success

Appointment booked

Your appointment has been confirmed for 15 July 2026 at 10:30am.

Success with a link

Success

Training outcome recorded and trainee withdrawn
