Where to find your NHS number

An NHS number is a 10 digit number, like 485 777 3456.

You can find your NHS number by logging in to a GP surgery online service or on any letter the NHS has sent you.

How to register with a GP surgery

You can register with any GP surgery as long as you live in the area. Most surgeries accept registrations online, by phone, or in person.

When registering you will need:

  • your name, address and date of birth
  • your NHS number (if you know it)
  • the name and address of your previous GP (if you had one)

Open by default

What happens after you apply

We will review your application within 5 working days. You will receive a confirmation email with your reference number.

Multiple details on a page

You can use multiple details components on a single page. They operate independently.

What is a prescription prepayment certificate?

A prescription prepayment certificate (PPC) lets you get as many NHS prescriptions as you need for a set price.

How much does it cost?

A 3-month PPC costs a fixed price. A 12-month PPC is also available at a different rate.

Who can get free prescriptions?

In Wales, all prescriptions are free of charge for patients registered with a Welsh GP.
