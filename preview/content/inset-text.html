Inset text

Use inset text to help users identify and understand important content on the page, even if they do not read the whole page. 

<div class="nhsw-inset-text">
  <p class="nhsw-body">Your important content here.</p>
</div>
{% from "inset-text/macro.njk" import nhswInsetText %}

{{ nhswInsetText({
  html: "<p class=\"nhsw-body\">Your important content here.</p>"
}) }}

Colour variants

Navy variant – nhsw-inset-text--navy

Blue variant – nhsw-inset-text--blue

Grey variant – nhsw-inset-text--grey

Red variant – nhsw-inset-text--red

Yellow variant – nhsw-inset-text--yellow

<!-- Colour modifier classes -->
