Components

Components are reusable building blocks that make up the interfaces of NHS Wales digital services. Each component has been designed and tested to meet accessibility standards and align with the DHCW design language.

Use components to build consistent, accessible pages without designing from scratch. Every component includes guidance on when and how to use it, coded examples, and accessibility considerations.

Components follow the NHS service manual and have been extended by Digital Health and Care Wales to meet the specific needs of Welsh health services. Where a component differs from the NHS baseline, guidance notes explain why.

Choosing the right component

Before using a component, read its guidance to understand when it is appropriate. Using the wrong component for a task can make services harder to use, particularly for people using assistive technology.

If you cannot find a component that meets your need, check the patterns section or speak to the DHCW design system team before building something new.
