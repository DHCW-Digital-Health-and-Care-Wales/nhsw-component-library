What's new

5 August 2026: DHCW Design System release

The Digital Health and Care Wales Design System is now live. Explore shared guidance, styles, components and patterns to design and build better digital services for NHS Wales.

Design principles

These principles guide our design. Use them to get started on a project and to help with making decisions.

Content guide

Use content standards and guidance to create clear, consistent and accessible content that users can understand and act on.

Styles

Use styles to create consistent and accessible user experiences, including colours, typography and icons.

Components

Components are reusable elements of the user interface.

Patterns

Patterns are tested and agreed solutions to common needs. You can reuse them across services to make them more consistent.

Support

The manual is maintained by the service design team at Digital Health and Care Wales. Contact us if you've got a question, idea or suggestion.
