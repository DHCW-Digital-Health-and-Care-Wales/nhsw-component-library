Warning callout

When to use a warning callout

Use a warning callout to help users identify very important content on the page, even if they do not read the whole page.

When not to use a warning callout

Do not use a warning callout if the information is not important enough. Use inset text or warning text instead.

How to use a warning callout

Give the callout a short, clearly worded heading. Keep the content concise, specific and self-contained.

Example

Important: Warning callout

Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit. Fusce at libero nisi. Suspendisse nec fringilla dolor, euismod dolor.

Specific heading example

Important: School, nursery or work

Stay away from school, nursery or work until you have not had symptoms for 2 days.
